Privacy policy

inbox-hub is a personal application used only by its owner to read and organise the owner's own email accounts.

Email data accessed through Google APIs is stored only on the owner's own computer. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Message content may be sent to an AI service solely to classify the owner's own mail.

inbox-hub's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
